                    Tor bridges
                    
                    
                

                

                    
Use pluggable transports when Tor itself is blocked. Needs the transport binary in the app’s tor-bin folder.

                    

                        
Use bridges
Route Tor through a pluggable transport.

                        
                    

                    

                        
                        

                            
                            
                        

                        
                    

                    

                        
                        
One per line, from bridges.torproject.org or Tor Browser. Snowflake needs its one bridge line too.

                        
                    

                    

                        
                        
                        
                    

                    

                        Where to get the files
                        Copy obfs4proxy.exe (or lyrebird.exe) and snowflake-client.exe from Tor Browser’s PluggableTransports folder into this app’s tor-bin folder.
